# Act mark

The fountain-pen nib uses white hairlines, an A-shaped breather aperture and a dark rounded tile. `Act.png` is the master image. `../Act.icns` contains the macOS icon sizes. Rebuild it with `zsh scripts/build-icon.sh` from the repository root.

Created with the built-in imagegen tool on 9 November 2026. Three passes established the nib, added the app tile and cleared the exterior corners. No third-party logo was used as a reference.

## Prompt record

Initial direction: a front-facing fountain-pen nib, writing point down, broad sculpted shoulders, engraved gold, polished bevels, a precise ink slit and an A-shaped negative-space breather aperture. Detailed engraving should follow the nib structure while its silhouette remains recognisable at small sizes. No text, barrel, hands or extra symbols.

Icon refinement: preserve the engraved gold nib, broad shoulders and A aperture. Restore opaque gold and clean edges. Centre the nib at about 80 percent of the square canvas height on a near-black rounded macOS tile with a subtle bevel. No text or extra symbols.

Final edit: retain the complete tile and nib unchanged and opaque. Make only the exterior beyond the rounded tile transparent, with anti-aliased edges.

White hairline revision, built-in imagegen: replace the entire gold nib with a minimal white hairline drawing. Use slender white strokes for the outer contour, A-shaped aperture and central slit, with a few spare curved lines. Keep the nib interior dark. Remove gold, metallic rendering, engraving, hatching and highlights. Preserve the dark rounded tile, composition and transparent exterior corners. No text or extra objects.
